    What the colours and tones mean
    • Read-only — nothing moves money.
    • Low — a plain confirmation.
    • Medium — read back in full first.
    • High — your spoken passphrase is required.
    • Severe — passphrase, then a hold you can cancel.
    • Blocked — refused; nothing was sent.

    Each band also has its own tone, so the risk is audible before any words.
